# ADR-0027 — Manga emoji art, power aura for energy, bigger sticker text

**Status:** accepted · 2026-10-24 · amends [ADR-0020](0020-anchored-stage-and-manga-panels.md)
(energy is no longer neon) and [ADR-0026](0026-threshold-emoji-and-gif-quality.md) (sticker look)

## Context
After the next round on WhatsApp the owner asked for three things: when a message contains an
emoji, show it as a large manga-style drawing next to the bubble (at most three, even if the
message has more); replace the neon energy effect with a "power aura"; and a better-looking sticker.
The owner supplied nine manga emoji drawings, generated by an AI service.

## Decision
1. **Emoji art is theme data.** A theme may list `emoji_art`: each entry names a PNG file relative
   to the theme folder and the emoji that call it up (`themes/anime/emoji/`). Matching ignores the
   variation selector U+FE0F. `Theme::emoji_art_for` returns the distinct images in the order their
   emoji first appear in the message, at most `MAX_EMOJI_ART` (3). The emoji itself stays in the
   text (ADR-0026); the art is an extra.
2. **Images are registered, not bundled.** `kd-fx` does not embed the PNGs: the renderer decodes
   them once through `Renderer::add_image` (PNG, at most 1024 px a side), and the FFI exposes one
   function, `Animator::add_image(name, png)`, which the shells call per file after building the
   animator. Art whose image was never added is skipped. This keeps the core free of the art (which
   cannot be published yet, docs/legal.md L30) and lets each shell ship the files as assets.
3. **Motion.** `scene::sample_art` pops each image in turn (ease-out-back, 450 ms, 220 ms apart),
   then bobs it gently; `Motion::Gentle` halves the bob, `Motion::Reduced` shows it still. The
   overlay layers fade it with the animation; the sticker keeps it. Slots: top-left, bottom-right,
   top-right of the bubble, drawn over the manga ink and kept inside the frame.
4. **Energy is a power aura.** New motif `power`: layered yellow-orange flame tongues around the
   bubble, inked on the outside, breathing in size a few times per loop (brightness constant), a
   pale core line, a few lightning bolts, ink speed rays, a warm backdrop and ドドド / ゴゴゴ!!.
   The `neon` motif stays available to themes but the anime theme no longer uses it.
5. **Bigger sticker text.** The message is drawn at 0.082 of the size unit instead of 0.058; long
   messages still shrink to fit.

## Consequences
- WhatsApp re-encodes shared GIFs (smaller palette and size); our output stays sharp, so part of the
  blur seen in the chat is out of our hands. Sharing an MP4 is a possible later step.
- Golden frames for energy and every sticker were regenerated on purpose.
- The art is AI-generated: shipping it publicly is blocked by docs/legal.md L30 (debug builds only).
